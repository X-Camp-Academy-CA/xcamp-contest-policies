在读学生竞赛奖励兑换政策
Current Student Contest Credit Redemption Policy

X-Camp 一直鼓励在读学生积极参与各类竞赛，将比赛作为学习成长的重要组成部分。为表彰学生的竞赛成就，同时考虑到部分学生持有的竞赛奖励凭证在原有政策下已无法正常兑换，X-Camp 特别决定向当前在读学生开放专属兑换通道。

X-Camp has always encouraged current students to actively participate in competitions as part of their learning journey. In recognition of students' contest achievements, and given that certain contest credits issued under previous policies may no longer be redeemable under standard terms, X-Camp has decided to extend a special redemption opportunity exclusively to currently enrolled students.

特别说明 · Please Note

本次特别兑换计划仅适用于2026 年外部竞赛中依据旧版获奖优惠政策所发放的竞赛奖励凭证。其他年度竞赛或依据不同政策发放的奖励凭证，均不在本次兑换范围之内。

This special redemption program applies exclusively to contest credits awarded under the previous winner credit policy for external contests in 2026 only. Credits from any other contest year or issued under a different policy are not eligible.

欢迎咨询班主任，现竞赛获奖新政策已针对新老生同时使用！ Feel free to consult your class coordinator — the new contest award policy now applies to both new and current students!
